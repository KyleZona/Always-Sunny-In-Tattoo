FAQs

How does booking work?

Tap Book Now and send a request with your idea, placement, size and any reference photos. Alexi takes one client a day, so she reviews every request personally, then sends back a price estimate and deposit info. Your date is locked in once the deposit is paid.

How does the deposit work?

The deposit is [$AMOUNT] and it goes toward the final price of your tattoo. It's non-refundable, but it can move with you once if you reschedule with at least 48 hours' notice.

Can I reschedule or cancel?

Life happens. Reschedule with 48+ hours' notice and your deposit carries over. Cancellations or no-shows with less than 48 hours' notice forfeit the deposit.

How should I prepare for my appointment?

Eat a real meal, drink plenty of water, get a good night's sleep and skip alcohol for 24 hours before. Wear comfy clothes that give easy access to the placement, and bring a valid photo ID.

Does it hurt?

Honestly? A little. Most people compare it to an annoying cat scratch. Ribs, feet and spine are spicier. Breaks are always allowed, and snacks are encouraged.

How do I take care of my new tattoo?

Keep the wrap on for [X hours], then wash gently with unscented soap and apply a thin layer of aftercare. No soaking, tanning or picking for 2–3 weeks. You'll leave with full written instructions.

Do you do cover-ups or touch-ups?

Yes to cover-ups: send a clear photo of the old tattoo with your request so Alexi can plan. Touch-ups on her own work are free within [3 months] of your appointment.

Do I need to be 18?

Yes. You must be 18 or older with a valid government-issued photo ID. No exceptions, even with a parent present.
